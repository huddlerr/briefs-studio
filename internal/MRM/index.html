The Math of This Story

December 22, 2025

The Departure

"The moment I knew I had to leave was when I realized my growth was dependent on my own agency, not the validation of anyone else."

I worked for 10 years in a position where my value was determined by my outputs. How many videos, graphics, scripts, frameworks, blueprints I could create within a given window of time.

I flourished externally until the soulless hustle burned me out in yearly cycles. And then even my outputs suffered—a lack of cohesion, care, spirit. The work was always someone else's, always on someone else's schedule. Success contingent on someone else's opinion.

After a decade measuring up to systems I'd grown within, it was time to build my own. Or rather—to see and accept that I had always been building. Just without knowing it.

What I Actually Do

I help people see and say what they actually mean—so they can stop optimizing for things that don't matter and start coordinating around things that do.

I started doing this because I noticed that most people, organizations, leaders, get so caught up in the outputs—the things external to them—that they miss entirely the actual story they're playing out. And subsequently they miss the truth within what they create, lead, and do.

The Math

How did we get here?

Our stories have been replaced by our data.

People are hungry for meaning amidst the wasteland of metrics, outputs, KPIs. Our world is full of noise, and the only way out is through—driven by authentic clarity and collective truth.

I see so many people and organizations struggling to articulate what they know is true, at the hands of increasing market demand. Fragmented narratives sabotaging real purpose.

This is for:

  • → The ones who feel the rising tension between what they're told to believe and their felt sense of what's true
  • → Who care about things passionately but struggle to express the why
  • → Who are fed up with "supposed to" and ready to map their own uncertain possible futures
  • → Who are ready to take their stories back

Questions.
Answered.

The thing people don't understand is how it could be possible to simply talk—and then experience revelation.

How can a 90-minute conversation change anything?

Because you already know what you need to say. You just can't hear it yet.

I'm not bringing you answers. I'm helping you recognize patterns that are already there—making the invisible visible. The map I create in real-time becomes a mirror that shows you what you've been trying to articulate.

What if I don't know what I need?

That's the perfect place to start. Most people come with a surface problem and discover the real one through conversation. The methodology is designed for exactly this—helping you articulate what you can't yet name.

Is Sleepy Planet therapy?

No. It's guided imagination—creating symbolic landscapes that reveal patterns you already know but can't articulate. Think collaborative storytelling where you're both the author and the explorer. The landscape becomes a map of your own knowing.

What's the "100 Strangers" project?

An ongoing research project—guided imagination sessions with 100 people I've never met. Free sessions for those willing to contribute to the research. The patterns emerging across these landscapes are becoming a map of their own. Currently at 10/100.
