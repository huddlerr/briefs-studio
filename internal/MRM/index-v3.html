Your questions.
Answered.

Not sure what to expect? These answers might help you feel more confident as you begin.

What happens in a Clarity Sprint?

We spend 90 minutes in conversation. I map in real-time on a shared screen. You watch your narrative emerge — patterns you couldn't see before become visible. You leave with a visual story map and the language you've been looking for.

How is Sleepy Planet different from therapy?

It's not therapy. It's guided imagination — creating symbolic landscapes that reveal patterns you already know but can't articulate. Think of it as collaborative storytelling where you're both the author and the explorer.

What if I don't know what I need?

That's actually the perfect place to start. The methodology is designed for exactly this — helping you articulate what you can't yet name. We begin with what you have, and clarity emerges through the conversation.

What does "100 Strangers" mean?

It's an ongoing research project — guided imagination sessions with 100 people I've never met. Free sessions for those willing to contribute to the research. The patterns emerging across these landscapes are becoming a map of their own.

Can organizations use Sleepy Planet?

Yes. Group sessions work beautifully for team retreats, leadership offsites, or anywhere a shared symbolic language would help a team see themselves differently. The methodology scales while keeping its intimate, revelatory quality.
