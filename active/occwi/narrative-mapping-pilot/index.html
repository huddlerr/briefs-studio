01 // CONTEXT

The Dr. Galbreath narrative map landed with leadership immediately. What typically requires weeks of interviews, drafts, and revisions became a strategic artifact in under 48 hours — one that communicated itself without needing a meeting to explain it.

This proposal specifies how we'll apply that same methodology within Story Capture.

OCCWI has people with critical knowledge, strategic vision, and hard-won expertise — but no efficient way to extract it, synthesize it, and package it into artifacts that actually land with leadership.

The Extraction Barrier

The Clarity Gap

Even when extraction occurs, the form rarely moves people. This methodology solves both.
