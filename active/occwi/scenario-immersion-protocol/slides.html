The Challenge

01

The Knowledge Trap

Information is not behavior.

Standard training assumes a knowledge deficit. But knowledge isn't the barrier.

Barrier 01 Social pressure and peer dynamics
Barrier 02 Fear of consequences or judgment
Barrier 03 Disconnect between intent and action

Why This Matters Now

• IPPWs need tools that actually work, not more content
• Prevention metrics aren't moving with current approaches
• Zero budget impact makes this immediately testable
• Success creates proof of concept for innovation
02

The Solution

Scenario Immersion Protocol (SIP)

"Rather than transmitting information through lectures, SIP creates structured experiences where service members discover their own behavioral patterns."

Sample Scenario: Barracks Weekend

Pilot Build

Peer Intervention Context

"A weekend evening in the barracks. Delta is showing signs of crisis. How does the group orient to the signals?"

Participants

4-6 Members using anonymous codenames (Alpha, Bravo, Charlie, etc.)

Sample Choice Point (01)
  • A Check in directly with Delta
  • B Mention to a friend quietly
  • • assumes handling it alone
Guided Choice-making reveals actual Behavioral Patterns
