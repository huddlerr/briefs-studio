01 // The Thesis

[Executive Summary]

Mycelium is the connective tissue between brands, artists, and their shared communities.

We've moved past the era of the extraction-based agency. Mycelium operates like the natural world: projects scale when resources are shared, artists thrive when they retain ownership, and brands succeed when their impact is authentic.

Distributed Growth

Instead of a central office, we scale through "cells"—regional networks of creatives who adopt the Mycelium methodology while maintaining local autonomy.

The IP Revolution

Artists keep their rights. Mycelium gets distribution. Brands get results. Everyone wins because ownership isn't zerosum.

04 // Risk & Resilience

Risk: Founder Burnout

Mitigation: Delegate producer role by Month 18. Structured sabbaticals + coaching.

Mitigation: Clear Values Doc. Artist veto power. Willingness to fire misaligned clients.

Mitigation: Open-source the methodology. Compete on execution quality and trust.
