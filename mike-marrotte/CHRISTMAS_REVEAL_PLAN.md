# 🎄 Dad's Christmas Brief Reveal Plan

## The Setup

**Christmas Day** — You're on the phone with Dad.

---

## Phase 1: The Email

Send a simple email with the link. Something like:

> **Subject:** Merry Christmas, Dad 🎄
>
> Hey Dad —
>
> Made you something. Check it out when you get a sec:
> **[link to brief]**
>
> Talk soon.
> — Mike

---

## Phase 2: The Live Reaction

**Stay on the phone.** Let him open the email and click the link.

Listen to him react to:
- The "Every Seat at the Table" positioning
- The circuit diagram of his career
- The testimonials
- The services section

**Don't mention the code yet.** Let him take it all in.

---

## Phase 3: The Reveal

After he's done reacting (probably saying something like "This is incredible" or "How did you do this?"), casually say:

> *"Oh hey — one more thing. I sent you a code in that email. Did you see it?"*

If you didn't put it in the email, send a follow-up text:

> **The Code:** ↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A
>
> (That's: Up Up Down Down Left Right Left Right B A on your keyboard)

Then say:

> *"Go to the page. Type that code."*

---

## The Code

```
↑ ↑ ↓ ↓ ← → ← → B A
```

On a keyboard:
- **Arrow keys:** Up, Up, Down, Down, Left, Right, Left, Right
- **Letters:** B, A

---

## What He'll See

A modal will pop up with:

> **A Note From Your Son**
>
> **Merry Christmas, Dad**
>
> *"32 years of building something real. Every relationship. Every deal. Every early morning and late night. I've watched you do it with integrity, humor, and heart."*
>
> This brief is my way of showing the world what I've always known — that you're one of the greats. Not because of the titles or the numbers, but because of how you treat people along the way.
>
> **Proud to be your son. 🎄**

---

## Why This Will Hit

1. He just spent 5 minutes being professionally impressed
2. He thinks the experience is over
3. Then BAM — the emotional payload
4. And you're there to hear it live
