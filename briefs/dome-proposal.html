1.0 Objective

Briefs Studio shall act as the Narrative Architect for the CISA CDM capture effort. The objective is to architect the winning narrative, translate it into a compelling video asset, and provide creative direction to ensure strategic alignment across capture, marketing, and creative execution teams.

2.0 Scope of Services & Deliverables

The Consultant (Mike Marrotte) will perform the following:

Phase 1: Narrative Foundation (Weeks 1-2)

  • Synthesize existing materials (e.g., workshop outputs, strategy docs).
  • Facilitate alignment sessions with key stakeholders (Capture, Marketing, SMEs, and Mighty Union where applicable).
Deliverable 1: Narrative Framework Document — A deck defining the core win story, proof points, and themes.

Phase 2: Video Architecture (Weeks 2-3)

  • Develop video concept, script, and storyboard based on the approved Narrative Framework.
  • Ensure storyboard demonstrates "Interactive Cyber Defense"—real-time, host-level visibility rather than static compliance reporting.
  • Incorporate "Secure by Design" principles into visual messaging.
  • Present and iterate on creative concepts.
Deliverable 2: Approved Video Script & Storyboard.

Phase 3: Production Oversight (Weeks 4-6)

  • Act as Creative Director for the VCS production team (Fern Ramos, et al.).
  • Lead review sessions and provide feedback on design/animation.
  • Ensure final video aligns with approved narrative and storyboard.
Deliverable 3: Final Video Assets.

  • SAIC will provide timely access to key personnel for interviews and reviews.
