The Environment Principle

The insight Megan and Elizabeth keep driving: it's not about individual risk factors or protective factors. It's about creating conditions where those factors can shift naturally. Conversations that don't look like leader-to-subordinate. Just conversations.

This mirrors how she works. Don't control the creative output. Create the conditions where good creative can emerge. Staff the right people, run the process, translate the client's chaos into actionable briefs.
