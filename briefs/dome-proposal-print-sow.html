Statement of Work

Briefs Studio — Narrative Architecture Services
For: SAIC, Civilian Markets Group
Project: CISA CDM Capture Video Project — Narrative Development & Video Production
Period of Performance: February 10 – March 24, 2026 (6 weeks)
Total Firm Fixed Price: $35,000 USD

1.0 Objective

Briefs Studio shall act as the Narrative Architect for the CISA CDM capture effort. The objective is to architect the winning narrative, translate it into a compelling video asset, and provide creative direction to ensure strategic alignment across capture, marketing, and creative execution teams.

2.0 Scope of Services & Deliverables

The Consultant (Mike Marrotte) will perform the following:

Phase 1: Narrative Foundation (Weeks 1-2)

  • Synthesize existing materials (e.g., workshop outputs, strategy docs).
  • Facilitate alignment sessions with key stakeholders (Capture, Marketing, SMEs, and Mighty Union where applicable).
Deliverable 1: Narrative Framework Document — A deck defining the core win story, proof points, and themes.

Phase 2: Video Architecture (Weeks 2-3)

  • Develop video concept, script, and storyboard based on the approved Narrative Framework.
  • Ensure storyboard demonstrates "Interactive Cyber Defense"—real-time, host-level visibility rather than static compliance reporting.
  • Incorporate "Secure by Design" principles into visual messaging.
  • Present and iterate on creative concepts.
Deliverable 2: Approved Video Script & Storyboard.

Phase 3: Production Oversight (Weeks 4-6)

  • Act as Creative Director for the VCS production team (Fern Ramos, et al.).
  • Lead review sessions and provide feedback on design/animation.
  • Ensure final video aligns with approved narrative and storyboard.
Deliverable 3: Final Video Assets.

3.0 Key Assumptions

  • SAIC will provide timely access to key personnel for interviews and reviews.
  • SAIC's internal VCS team (led by Fern Ramos) will handle production execution. VCS production costs are separate and will be estimated collaboratively based on approved creative direction.
  • All necessary background IP will be provided by SAIC.

4.0 Payment

Milestone Timing Amount
SOW Signing & Kick-off Upon execution $35,000

Single payment due upon SOW execution. Invoice Net 15. Invoice shall be submitted to Linda Hanus and Fern Ramos. Note: Production costs for video execution (animation, editing, rendering) by VCS team are separate and will be invoiced directly by VCS based on approved scope.

5.0 Governance & Acceptance

  • Primary SAIC POCs: Meng Wengzhu (Capture Lead), Linda Hanus & Fern Ramos (VCS).
  • Weekly 30-minute alignment call.
  • Incremental Acceptance: Each phase deliverable requires formal sign-off before the next phase begins. Deliverables are deemed accepted within 5 business days unless specific, written feedback is provided.
  • Locked Narrative Baseline: Formal acceptance of Phase 1 (Narrative Foundation) creates the "Locked Narrative Baseline." Any deviations requested after Phase 2 begins that contradict this baseline will be handled as a change in scope.

6.0 Terms & Intellectual Property

This is a firm fixed-price engagement. The Consultant is an independent contractor. IP: All final deliverables (Narrative Framework, Storyboard, Video Assets) become SAIC property upon acceptance. Consultant retains rights to proprietary workflows and methodologies.
