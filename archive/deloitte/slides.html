02

The Process

Applied strategic communications in four steps:

01

Strategic Listening

Facilitated conversation or existing recording. Structured note-taking focused on extracting patterns, priorities, and themes.

02

Pattern Synthesis

Identifying key themes, recurring priorities, and strategic frameworks from the conversation. Professional judgment applied to structure the narrative.

03

Visual Design

Creating professional strategic documents using design tools. Custom layouts, clear information hierarchy, brand-appropriate execution.

04

Veracity Review

Subject reviews output for accuracy. Revisions incorporated. Final deliverable approved before sharing.

03

Tools & Approach

Current Toolkit

  • → Design Software: Professional design tools for visual execution (Adobe Suite, Figma)
  • → AI Augmentation: Consumer AI tools for synthesis assistance and note-taking efficiency
  • → Web Development: VS Code for creating interactive web-based deliverables

Key Point: AI tools are used the same way design software is used—as augmentation for professional judgment, not replacement. The strategic thinking and design decisions remain mine.

Data Handling

  • ✓ No PII included in any AI prompts
  • ✓ Work stays in personal workspace
  • ✓ Final deliverables are PDFs/web pages
  • ✓ Nothing integrated with Deloitte systems
  • ✓ Subject veracity review before publication

Consent & Review Protocol

Proposed workflow for compliant delivery:

1

Recording Consent

Participant signs consent form before any recorded session. Clear explanation of how content will be used.

2

Synthesis on Approved Tools

All AI-assisted synthesis performed on DoD-approved platforms. No PII in prompts. No external data sharing.

3

Veracity Review

Subject reviews draft output for accuracy. Opportunity to correct, clarify, or remove content before finalization.

4

Approved Publication

Final sign-off before any sharing. Deliverables stay within appropriate channels.
